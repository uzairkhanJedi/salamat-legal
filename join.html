  1. Install Outing Already installed? Skip to step 2.
    Get it on Google Play (Android) Get it on iPhone (TestFlight)
    Android: tap “Become a tester”, then “Download it on Google Play”. The store page is called “com.uzairkhan.salamat (unreviewed)” — that’s the app. Use the same Google account as your Play Store. If it says “Item not found”, send me the Gmail address on that phone and I’ll add it.

Continue with Apple and Continue with Google work too, if you prefer. ایپ کھولیں، اپنا ای میل لکھیں، اور ای میل میں آنے والا 6 ہندسوں کا کوڈ لکھیں
  3. Join the outing with the code above
    Open in Outing · ایپ کھولیں
    The button fills the code in for you. Nothing happened? Open Outing yourself, tap Join an outing, type the six characters, then tap Ask to join.
    If it then says “Request sent — waiting for … to approve”, that is normal: I approve you and you’re in. If I sent you a personal invite link instead, just tap that link — it takes you straight in. اوپر والا کوڈ لکھیں؛ اگر «Request sent» لکھا آئے تو یہ ٹھیک ہے، لیڈر منظوری دے گا
  4. Say yes when it asks for location Choose “Allow all the time” (iPhone: “Always Allow”) so family can find you even when your screen is off. iPhone asks a second time a few days later — choose Keep Always. لوکیشن کی اجازت میں «ہر وقت» چنیں

Your location is visible only to the people in your outing, and only while it is running — never sold, never shared. Location history deletes itself after 7 days. If the leader switches on route history for an outing, that outing’s replay is kept for the period they choose (7–90 days) and you can delete your own track at any time. آپ کی لوکیشن صرف آپ کے گروپ کو، اور صرف آؤٹنگ کے دوران نظر آتی ہے۔ سات دن بعد ریکارڈ خود مٹ جاتا ہے۔
