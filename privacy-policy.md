# Salamat — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 19 July 2026**

Salamat is a location-sharing app for families and small groups travelling together
for Hajj and Umrah. It exists so the people in your group can see where each other
are at the holy sites. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, who can
see it, and how long we keep it.

Salamat is operated by Uzair Khan. Contact: **info@shocute.com**

## What we collect

**Location, including in the background.** Salamat records your device's precise
location (latitude and longitude) and sends it to your group. Collection continues
when the app is closed or not in use — this is the core purpose of the app. Without
background location, your family would only see you while you had the app open,
which would make it useless for its intended purpose.

**Location history.** Each location reading is stored as a historical record, not
just your latest position.

**Battery level.** Your device's battery percentage is shared with your group, so
they can tell the difference between "stopped moving" and "phone died".

**Profile information.** Your phone number, your display name, your chosen language,
and — only if you choose to upload one — a profile photo.

**Push notification token.** A device identifier issued by Firebase Cloud Messaging,
used to deliver notifications to your device.

**Diagnostics.** Crash reports and error diagnostics, collected via Sentry, to fix
bugs. These may include your app version, device model, and operating system version.

## Why we collect it

We use this data only to operate the app:

- To show your location to the other members of your group.
- To notify you about meeting points and group activity.
- To keep the app working, and to diagnose crashes.

**We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.
We do not use it for advertising or profiling. We do not use your location for any
purpose other than showing it to your own group.**

## Who can see your data

**Your location, battery level, name, and photo are visible to the other members of
the group you join.** That is the point of the app. They are not visible to the
public, and they are not visible to members of other groups.

Your data is stored on a private server operated by us on Google Cloud
infrastructure. The following third parties process limited data on our behalf:

- **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging** — delivers push notifications.
- **Sentry** — receives crash and error diagnostics.
- **Google Cloud** — hosts the server and database.

We disclose data to no one else, except where we are legally required to.

## Profile photos are stored publicly

If you upload a profile photo, it is stored in a **publicly readable** storage
bucket. The web address is hard to guess, but it is not protected by a password or
login. Anyone who has the address can view the photo. Please do not upload a photo
you would not be comfortable being seen outside your group.

## How long we keep it

**Your location history is automatically deleted after 7 days.** A scheduled job on
our server permanently drops location records older than that, every day. We keep a
short window only so the app can tell your group leader whose phone has stopped
reporting, and so we can diagnose problems during a trip.

Your current location (your latest position, the one on the map) is kept until it is
replaced by a newer one, or until your account is deleted.

Your profile information is kept for as long as your account exists.

## Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your account and all associated data — including your entire
location history — by emailing **info@shocute.com** from the phone number or with the
details associated with your account. We will action the request within 30 days.

You can also delete your account yourself from inside the app, in Settings. See
[Delete Your Account](https://uzairkhanjedi.github.io/salamat-legal/delete-account.html)
for exactly what is removed, what is kept, and how to ask.

## Children

Salamat is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data
from them.

## Permissions Salamat requests

- **Location (including background)** — to share your position with your group. Required.
- **Notifications** — to alert you about meeting points and group activity.
- **Camera and photo library** — only if you choose to set a profile photo or attach
  a photo to a meeting point.
- **Physical activity / motion** — to reduce battery use by slowing location updates
  when you are not moving.
- **Ignore battery optimisations** — so Android does not suspend location sharing in
  the background. This is what keeps your family's map from silently going stale.
