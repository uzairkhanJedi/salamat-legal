Salamat — Delete Your Account

Last updated: 17 July 2026

You can delete your Salamat account and the data attached to it at any time. You do not need to have the app installed to make the request. This page explains how to ask, exactly what gets deleted, and what is kept.

Salamat is operated by Uzair Khan. Contact: info@shocute.com

How to request deletion

Email info@shocute.com with the subject “Delete my Salamat account”, and include:

  • The phone number registered to your account, and
  • The display name your group sees on the map.

We use those two details to find your account. Because deletion is permanent, we will then confirm the request with you on that phone number before we delete anything — the people in your group already know your name and number, so an email on its own is not proof the request came from you. If we cannot match your details to an account, or we cannot reach you to confirm, we will write back and ask. We will not delete an account on a guess.

We will action your request within 30 days of receiving it, and email you to confirm once it is done. In practice it is usually the same week. There is no charge, and you do not have to give a reason.

Deleting from inside the app

If you still have Salamat installed, you can delete your account yourself without emailing anyone: open Settings, scroll to the bottom, and tap Delete account. You will be asked to confirm twice, and it takes effect immediately.

The in-app button removes your account record and everything that hangs off it — your group membership, your current location, and your full location history. It does not remove your profile photo or the anonymous sign-in record described below. If you want those removed too, email us — the email route covers everything on this page.

What gets deleted

When we action your request, the following are permanently deleted from our live server — not hidden or deactivated, but erased. Please also read what is kept below, which is short and honest about the exceptions.

  • Your profile — phone number, display name, language preference, and push notification token.
  • Your location history — every stored location reading, in full.
  • Your current location — the position shown on your group's map, and the battery level shared alongside it.
  • Your group membership — you are removed from your group. Other members stop seeing you on the map immediately.
  • Your alerts — the alerts you exchanged with your group leader, in both directions, and any announcements you sent to the group.
  • Your profile photo — the image file itself is removed from storage.
  • Your sign-in record — the anonymous account Salamat created for you when you first joined with an invite code.

What is kept, and for how long

We want to be straight with you about the small amount that does not disappear:

  • Crash diagnostics — up to 90 days. If the app crashed while you were using it, the crash report is stored by our error-reporting provider (Sentry) and is tagged with your account's random internal ID. These reports contain your app version, device model, and operating system — never your name, phone number, photo, or location. They age out automatically within 90 days. Once your account is deleted, the ID they carry no longer points to anything.
  • Things you created for your group — kept, but no longer linked to you. If you set a meeting point, or you created the group itself, that content stays so the rest of your group does not lose it. Your name is unlinked from it, so it is no longer attributable to you. Nothing here contains your location or your contact details.
  • Announcements your group leader sent you — kept. These belong to your leader's account rather than yours, so deleting your account does not remove them. If your leader wrote your name into the text of an announcement, that text stays until your leader deletes it or deletes their own account.
  • One maintenance backup, taken 5 July 2026. We took a single snapshot of our server that day while moving it, and it has not been deleted yet. It is a frozen copy: it is not connected to the app, nobody can read it from the app, and it is never used to restore your profile or put you back on a map. But we have to be straight with you — deleting your account does not erase you from that snapshot, and it contains location history from before July 2026. We are deleting the snapshot itself; once it is gone, this entry will be removed from this page and nothing identifying you will remain.
Apart from the 5 July 2026 snapshot described above — which we are deleting — nothing we keep after your request can be used to identify you, contact you, or work out where you have been.

If you just want to stop sharing your location

Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone — you would need a new invite code to rejoin. If you only want to stop sharing your position, you can turn Location sharing off in Settings, or leave your group, and keep your account. Your location history is deleted automatically after 7 days either way.
